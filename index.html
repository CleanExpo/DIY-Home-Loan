Welcome

FITTER is a practical, step-by-step approach to financial fitness: discipline, support, and educated guesses over endless analysis. The DIY Home Loan — the Mortgage to Wealth Machine — pairs a principal home loan (GISI) with an investment line of credit (LOC) and twenty annual rules so discipline holds through market cycles. It is for households moving from a loan that feels stuck to a self-driving plan for debt, wealth, and retirement.

FITTER Framework

The FITTER model aligns six planning pillars so decisions in one area support the others. From the book's perspective, this is about practical control: simple, consistent decisions you understand and can sustain.

Finance

Understanding income, expenses, budgeting, and saving. Know where your money is, how your budget works, and what your debt is doing, with simple structures you can maintain.

Insurance

Protecting what matters most. Review life, TPD, trauma, income protection and general insurance regularly so your household remains resilient as life and responsibilities change.

Taxation

Working with the system efficiently. Make tax planning proactive, keep records current, lodge on time, and align strategy with professional advice to reduce avoidable leakage.

Trusts

Advanced wealth protection strategies. Trusts are about clarity before complexity, with ownership and control structures aligned to family and business objectives.

Estate planning

Planning for your legacy. A clear, valid will and current legal documents protect loved ones, reduce conflict, and ensure your intentions are carried through.

Retirement planning

Building wealth for the future. Define what retirement means for you early, then build toward it with disciplined contributions, compounding, and realistic long-term income planning.

Before Fitzy can help with your budget

Fitzy needs you to work with the process. The budget is only ever as good as you allow it to be — honest inputs and a simple routine beat a perfect spreadsheet you never update.

Tip 1 — build a download habit (bank + cards every month or quarter):

  • Bank statements (every account you use)
  • Credit card statements (every card)
  • Payslips or other income evidence (self-employed / trust: a clear household summary)
  • Cash spends and cash income (often the hidden gap)
  • Family loans — money in, repayments out
  • Known future spends — holidays, medical, car, insurance renewals, school, gifts

Every great journey starts with a step in the right direction. Your first budget will not be perfect — neither will your 5th or your 10th — life brings improvements and setbacks. We just keep refining each quarter.

Start here on this page: connect Open Banking so Fitzy can pull transactions and build your quarterly budget for you — you do not type the monthly budget by hand. Use the Connect Open Banking card next.

Connect Open Banking — send data to Fitzy

If you applied for a home loan through a broker, you may already have used Frollo for Brokers. For your ongoing quarterly DIY budget, the usual path is the consumer app (Frollo for You) so you can keep accounts linked under Consumer Data Right and share what Fitzy needs.
